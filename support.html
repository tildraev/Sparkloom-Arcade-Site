Support

Need help with Sparkloom Arcade or one of its games? Open a support request and include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, game name, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording is helpful when available.

How do I return to the arcade?

Open a game’s gear menu and choose Return to Sparkloom Arcade.

How do I replay a tutorial?

Open that game’s gear menu and choose Replay Tutorial.

Where is my progress stored?

Your unlocked games, levels, best scores, tutorial status, and settings are stored locally on your device. Removing the app also removes this progress.

Why isn’t an ad available?

Ads require a network connection and inventory from the advertising provider. If a rewarded ad cannot load, try again later. A continue or boost is granted only after the reward is earned.

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Open the gear menu and select Privacy Options when that option is displayed for your region.
