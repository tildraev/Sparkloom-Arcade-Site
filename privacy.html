Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

ChromaCairn stores your current level, high score, tutorial status, and settings locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account system or server that receives this game data.

Advertising

ChromaCairn uses Google Mobile Ads to show interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, coarse location, advertising data, product interactions, diagnostics, and performance data to provide, measure, and protect advertising, depending on your region and privacy choices.

Where required, ChromaCairn asks for your advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. You can revisit those choices through Settings → Privacy Options whenever that control is required and available. You can also manage tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Learn more from Google’s Privacy Policy and its explanation of advertising technologies.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains until you delete the app or clear it by reinstalling. Advertising providers control retention and deletion of data they process under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit ChromaCairn Support.
